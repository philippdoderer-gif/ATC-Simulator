Four positions

Delivery & Ground

IFR clearances with SID and squawk, start-up, pushback with facing direction, taxi routes built taxiway by taxiway, de-icing in frost.

Tower

Line-up, take-off and landing clearances, runway crossings, go-arounds, wake turbulence timer between departures.

Center

Radar with real data blocks, vectors to the ILS, climbs to the exit fix, overflights, thunderstorm cells to steer around.

Tower + Ground

The combined night position: one controller for the whole airport, from stand to runway and back.

What makes it real

  • Real layouts. Taxiways, aprons, terminals and stands from OpenStreetMap.
  • Real phraseology. Pilots read back – sometimes wrong. Catch the squawk.
  • Wake turbulence. 2 min behind a Heavy, 3 min behind an A380, 4–7 NM on final.
  • Weather. ATIS changes, low visibility, frost and de-icing, thunderstorms.
  • Daily traffic curve. Morning and evening banks, quiet nights, night restrictions.
  • Emergencies. MAYDAY and PAN PAN calls that need priority.

How to play

  1. Pick an airport, a position and a start hour above.
  2. Read the briefing: runways, weather, first departures, NOTAMs.
  3. Tap an aircraft or strip and choose an instruction. Glowing aircraft are calling you.
  4. Use Coach if you are unsure what comes next, and 2×–16× in quiet periods.
  5. End shift for your grade and best score per airport.
